Our maps

Country maps and capital questions reuse Rufus Trivia’s existing dataset and question generators.

Country outlines: Natural Earth (public domain), via world-atlas. Country and capital data: flag-icons (MIT). Map rendering: d3-geo and topojson-client (ISC).

US states: US Census Bureau 2017 cartographic boundaries via us-atlas 3.0.1 (ISC). Alaska and Hawaii appear as insets, not at the same scale as the contiguous states. DC and territories are not included in the 50-state quiz.

The little globe in the room is decorative; quiz maps use actual geographic outlines. The Capital Quest map highlights the country, not the exact location of the city.
