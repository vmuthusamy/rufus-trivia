    Grown-up design notes and proposed numbers

    What's decided, and what isn't

    Confirmed

    • Packs describe themes. Rarity belongs to cards.
    • Common, Rare, Epic, Divine, Legendary, with Legendary the hardest.
    • Rufus Family pack: Renard Common, Martina Rare, Fiery and Felix Epic, Rufus Divine, all five together Legendary.
    • Flag pack: characters hold a country's flag. Its Legendary is Rufus with the big flag poster.
    • Right answers earn coins, and coins buy packs. Earned only.
    • Existing stickers and progress are kept.
    • Cards show on leaderboards and in multiplayer, and give no scoring, timing or answer advantage.

    Suggested

    • 10 coins a right answer, plus 20 for finishing a game.
    • 100 coins a pack, 3 cards a pack, and at most 300 coins a day.
    • The Rufus Family odds shown on the Inspect screen.
    • One new card in each pack, from Common to Epic only.
    • A Legendary by pack 50 at the latest.
    • 10 spares could make a foil of a card you already own.
    • A free starter card, so everyone can feature something.
    • Stickers as up to 3 reusable stamps.
    • Hide featured cards during questions, because a Japan card could give away a Japan question.

    Undecided

    • Other Flag-card rarities. The Flag examples show "Rarity undecided" on purpose.
    • Which character holds which flag. These pairings are only examples.
    • Flag pack odds, apart from a proposed 0.5% for the poster.
    • How many Flag cards the pack holds.
    • Fiery holding a flag. Fiery has no raised paw in the sprite, so a Flag card needs a small art decision.

    Short recommendation

    Earning pace

    The normal draw odds are now Rufus 1% and Legendary 0.5%. With three normal draws, a pack has about a 3% chance of Rufus and a 1.5% chance of Legendary. While one slot guarantees a missing Common–Epic, those chances are about 2% and 1%.

    The proposed 50-pack Legendary guarantee is separate. We have not measured how many games or days earning a pack takes.

    Duplicates

    With only 6 cards, duplicate cards will be common. Pool them toward a foil of a card you own (not built yet), as Codex suggested. Spares should never buy a Divine or Legendary card, or those stop being hard to get.

    Keeping stickers special

    Stickers stay out of packs, so they're always proof of something you did. Stamps are copies, so nothing is used up, and every old sticker still works. Tapping a stamp could show how it was earned.

    Three things to settle

    Martina’s name

    The design prompt says Martina, and so does Adventures of Rufus. The current trivia game uses the spelling Marthina in some places. This preview follows the owner: Martina. Existing game identifiers and saved collections have not been renamed.

    A new-card guarantee can break Legendary

    If every pack guarantees a new card of any rarity, a 6-card pack gives out Legendary in about 3 packs. Limiting the guarantee to Common through Epic keeps Legendary the hardest card to get.

    Flag rarity shouldn't rank countries

    A rare France and a common Nigeria would send the wrong message on a game for kids everywhere. Tie Flag rarity to the character, pose or scene, so every country can appear at every rarity. Five characters and six continent backgrounds would also cover all 195 flags without drawing each card by hand.
